### Added
- `lium exec --detach/-d` starts the command in the background on the pod (`nohup setsid bash -lc ... < /dev/null &`), prints its PID and log path, and returns immediately. `--log PATH` overrides the default `/workspace/logs/exec-<timestamp>.log`. With `--script` the script is copied to the pod first and then started detached. `--json` reports `pid`, `log` and `error` per pod.
