### Added
- `lium ls --country CODE|NAME` (repeatable or comma-separated), `--min-vram GB`, `--max-price USD` (per GPU-hour) and `--tier spot|secure`, applied client-side on the same values `--format json` prints. When filters leave nothing, the message names the filters instead of claiming every GPU is rented out.
- `lium ls --format json` rows gain `country_code`, `city` and `machine_name`.

### Changed
- `lium ls` explains the order and the ★ marker in one line under the table (cheapest `$/GPU·h` first; no other node is both faster to download and cheaper; `--sort` picks another key) and its `--help` has examples.
- README: the `lium ls H100` examples used a positional argument that does not exist; they now use `--gpu`.
