### Changed
- `lium ps` shows a `#` column and `--format json` carries `index`: the row number other commands accept as a pod target. `ps` records the listing (`~/.lium/last_ps.json`).
- `rm`, `ssh`, `exec`, `scp` and every other command that takes pod targets only accept a row number while that row still holds the pod the last `lium ps` showed there, and for 10 minutes after the listing. Otherwise they fail with `stale_pod_index` (exit 2) naming both pods, instead of acting on whatever pod the account-wide list now has in that row.
- `lium rm` prints what each row number resolved to (`1 → eager-wolf-aa (name: train)`) and, on a terminal without `--yes`, asks before removing pods selected by number.

### Added
- `lium rm --name-only` and `LIUM_NO_POD_INDEX=1` (all commands): treat numeric targets as names only.
