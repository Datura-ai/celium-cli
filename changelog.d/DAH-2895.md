### Changed
- The SDK retries transient failures (429, 5xx, network errors) only for `GET`/`HEAD`/`OPTIONS`. `POST`, `PUT`, `PATCH` and `DELETE` — template, volume and backup creation, restore, reboot, scheduled removal, pay-API transfers, every delete — are sent once by default, so a lost response no longer creates a duplicate or turns a completed delete into "not found". `_request(..., retry=True|False)` overrides the default per call.
