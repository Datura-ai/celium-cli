- SDK/CLI: `Lium.wait_ready()` and `lium up` poll every 2 s for the first 90 s of a wait and every 10 s after that (`poll_interval=None`, the new default; a fixed `poll_interval` is still honoured). A pod the backend marks RUNNING at ~22 s used to be reported up to 10 s later. A pod that never appears in the pod list is declared missing after 20 s (`Lium.MISSING_GRACE_SECONDS`) instead of after 3 checks, so the faster polling does not turn one listing hiccup into an error.
