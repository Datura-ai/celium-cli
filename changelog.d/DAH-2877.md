### Added
- `lium up` compares the pod's billed GPU count with the requested count (`--count`, or the chosen node's count) once the pod is ready and exits non-zero on a mismatch, naming both numbers and the node.
- `lium up --verify-gpus` additionally counts the GPUs `nvidia-smi` reports inside the pod over SSH and fails when it differs from the billed count.
- `lium up --strict-gpus` removes the pod automatically when a GPU-count mismatch is detected.
